# RISK REGISTER — IKS × Modern Science Integration

| ID | Category | Risk Description | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation Strategy | Verification Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | Scientific Accuracy | Conflating Acharya Kanada's philosophical *Paramanu* with the experimentally characterized Dalton/Bohr/Quantum atom | Critical | Medium | Hard-code explicit distinction in Domain A claims (`P1`/`H1` vs `S1`), UI banner, and assessment rationale | Automated test checks `evidenceClass` != `S1` for *Paramanu* claim and presence of non-equivalence disclaimer |
| **R2** | Medical Safety | Presenting Ayurvedic claims or home remedies as clinically validated treatments or medical diagnoses | Critical | Medium | Tag traditional claims honestly (`H1`/`T1`/`D1`/`U1`), include evidence-based clinical trial boundaries, prohibit diagnostic/prescriptive advice | Schema & content test asserts clinical/medical disclaimer in Domain C |
| **R3** | Chemical / Lab Safety | Demonstrating alcohol distillation, toxic solvents, open uncontrolled heating, or unsafe biological cultures | Critical | Low | Restrict Domain F experiments exclusively to non-hazardous water/salt-water and steam hydro-distillation models; include safety & teacher supervision fields | Test checks Domain F materials/procedures exclude hazardous reagents & alcohol |
| **R4** | Astronomy vs Astrology | Mixing astronomical timekeeping/eclipse geometry with predictive astrology | High | Medium | Separate mathematical astronomy (`H1`/`S1`) from astrological beliefs (`U1`); display explicit Astronomy vs Astrology distinction | Test verifies Domain G carries distinction flag & zero predictive astrology content |
| **R5** | Student Health Privacy | Storing personal pulse/breathing/health metrics from Yoga & Physiology activities | Critical | Medium | Domain D simulator uses parameterized physiological models only; `progress-service.js` explicitly blocks storing personal health metrics | Privacy test confirms schema & analytics never store biometric/health values |
| **R6** | Citation Integrity | Fabricated Sanskrit verses, manuscript dates, DOIs, or unverified claims | Critical | Low | Only cite verified primary text names, historically consensus eras, or mark unverified page/translation details `"Reference verification pending"`; show insufficiency message for weak evidence | Audit test verifies no invented DOI patterns & checks mandatory fields on every claim |
| **R7** | Curriculum Misrepresentation | Implying IKS historical/enrichment modules are compulsory CBSE board examination topics | High | Medium | Every item carries one of 5 strict CBSE scope labels; separate Class IX and Class X tabs; explicit non-compulsory exam notice | Curriculum test validates scope labels & Class IX/X separation |
| **R8** | Ecological Over-Generalization | Claiming Jharkhand / Santhal Pargana traditional practices work universally in all climates/soils | Medium | Medium | Embed regional Chota Nagpur plateau / Santhal Pargana context, soil/slope limits, and failure conditions in Domain H | Content check ensures contextual limits are present in Domain H |
| **R9** | Simulation vs Reality Confusion | Students mistaking simulated plots for real physical measurements | High | Medium | Every experiment displays Activity Type (`A`–`E`) and every visualization displays a schematic/simulation disclaimer | UI & experiment engine test enforces `activityType` and disclaimer string |
| **R10** | Performance & Bandwidth | Heavy assets slowing low-end rural/semi-urban mobile devices | High | Medium | Pure lightweight Canvas 2D / SVG visualizations; zero extra WebGL/GLB assets; lazy dialog rendering; self-hosted fonts already cached | Check total added JS/CSS asset weight and lazy initialization |
| **R11** | Linguistic & A11y Quality | Poor machine-translated Hindi terms or inaccessible controls | Medium | Medium | Hand-curated NCERT-aligned Hindi scientific glossary (`परमाणु`, `विद्युत-अपघटन`, `आसवन`, `संक्षारण`, etc.), ARIA roles, keyboard controls, high-contrast mode | Automated bilingual completeness check + keyboard handler verification |
| **R12** | App Regression / XSS | Breaking existing Vigyan Atlas 3D stage/simulators or introducing XSS via custom notes | High | Low | Feature-flagged additive UI, zero edits to core 3D/simulator/mindmap logic, HTML escaping on all rendered strings, `npm test` suite run after integration | Full existing `npm test` + new `tests/iks-science.js` suite |
